## The excuse

> My laptop charger died overnight and the spare's in my office drawer. Battery's at 4%.

One line a gamer texts the boss to get out of work during GTA 6
launch week. A boring, specific, almost believable workplace detail —
and the joke is in the excuse's own logic: the only fix is coming
into the office, which is exactly what the sender will not do.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
